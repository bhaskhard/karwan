About the Temple

Also remembered as Udyoga Hanuman, this devotional landmark is associated with the faith of devotees who seek Hanuman’s strength, courage and guidance.

According to local traditions presented in the supplied sample page, the shrine is associated with Samartha Ramdas Swami and a distinctive basement sanctum. These historical and devotional accounts should be locally verified.
