About the Temple

A temple dedicated to Sri Ranganatha Swamy, a reclining form of Lord Vishnu, in the Jiyaguda area of Hyderabad.

The temple offers an opportunity for devotees to reflect on Vishnu bhakti and the traditions surrounding Sri Ranganatha Swamy.
